Saving Memories 

About the Project
Saving Memories is a website designed for people who want to preserve their memories for a lifetime.
It allows users to look back on their past experiences in the future, revisit meaningful moments whenever they want, 
and continue adding new pictures as they create more memories. These memories may include personal achievements,
unforgettable moments with friends, special family gatherings, celebrations, and other important experiences in life. 


Features:
1. Home Page: In home page we will see a catchy or introducing the point of view of Saving Memories.
2. About Page: It tells on idea and purpose behind Saving Memories, focusing on the importance
of preserving special experiences and remembering meaningful moments.
3. Login Page: Commonly these features are the key to access your memories that you've insert in Saving Memories.
4. Registration Page: These are the first way to access the login page by typing your information that we need
so you can proceed to access the Login Page.
